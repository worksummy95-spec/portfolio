DROP YOUR DOWNLOADED CREATIVES HERE
===================================

Copy the contents of your two downloaded folders into these two subfolders:

  \aluminium\    ->  everything from @jindalaluminium
  \naturecure\   ->  everything from @jindalnaturecure

You do NOT need to rename anything. Put statics, videos and carousel images
in as-is. For a carousel (multi-image post), keep its images together and
name them so they sort in order, e.g.:
      post3_1.jpg, post3_2.jpg, post3_3.jpg
(any shared prefix + a number works).

Supported best: .jpg .png .webp (images) and .mp4 (video).
If you have .mov or .heic files, drop them in anyway — I'll convert them.

When done, tell me "added" and I'll read the folders and build the gallery
to show images, videos and carousels correctly.
